Herding / Information Cascade

新品会不会爆 Viral Product Guess

每一轮都会出现一款新品。它真实上可能大概率会火，也可能大概率不会火。
Each round introduces one new product. In truth it is either more likely to go viral or more likely to flop.

学生按顺序一个个判断。你能看到前面同学公开做出的判断，也会私下拿到自己的一条线索。难点在于：你是相信自己的线索，还是跟着前面的人走？
Students decide one by one. You see earlier public guesses and get one private signal of your own. The tension is whether to trust your own clue or follow the herd.
